## Texts

**Tagline:** Let project admins manage their own custom fields — safely, within your guidelines.

**Summary (≤ 250 characters):** Project admins add fields and dropdown options themselves, checked against your rules.
Jira admins approve what matters in one click, with a before/after preview. Every change is logged and can be undone.

**Highlights**

1. **Self-service with guard rails** — naming rules, reserved names, limits and a preview of the impact on work items
   before anything changes.
2. **Approvals where they matter** — requests land in your Jira Service Management space; Jira admins see exactly what
   changes and approve or reject in one click. Large changes need two people.
3. **Undo, audit log and field health** — who changed what, when and with whose approval; undo from the log; find
   unused, hidden and duplicate fields with clean-up suggestions.

**More details:** Field Assistant, a Rovo agent, prepares requests in a conversation and previews every change. Anyone
can request a field from a work item; project admins decide first. Site check with one-click fixes; daily self-test.
Runs on Atlassian: no data leaves your Atlassian site.
